- **Pointer 1: Analysis of the feasibility of the idea**
  - **Technical Feasibility:** Client-side execution, sub-second inference, lightweight 5.2 MB APK, zero heavy server requirements.
  - **Operational Feasibility:** Runs on standard Android smartphones of inspectors & citizens; zero specialized hardware needed.
  - **Legal Feasibility:** Strictly adheres to LMPC Act 2009 & FSSA 2006; dockets are admissible under Section 65B Evidence Act.
  - **Financial Viability:** Open-source core, ultra-low cloud API cost, massive fiscal recovery via automated state penalty enforcement.
- **Pointers 2 & 3: Potential challenges, risks and mitigation strategies**
  - **Challenge 1 (Optical Degradation & Glare):** Curved cans, shiny foil, crumpled packaging causing OCR misreads.
    - *Strategy:* Client-side HTML5 Canvas adaptive contrast normalization, 40% cylindrical surface wrap math, voice-assisted fallback.
  - **Challenge 2 (Rural & Offline Environments):** Tier-3 markets, weekly mandis lacking signal causing loss of connectivity.
    - *Strategy:* Offline-first SQLite & LocalStorage docket buffer in the native Android APK with automatic background sync upon reconnection.
  - **Challenge 3 (Adversarial Legal Contestations):** FMCG brands disputing algorithmic fines in Consumer Commissions.
    - *Strategy:* AI is restricted strictly to visual extraction; legal audits are 100% deterministic, sealed with SHA-256 cryptographic hashes and side-by-side photographic proof.
